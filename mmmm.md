🏆 3 KILLER FEATURES (Jo Koi Nahi De Raha)
1. 🤖 "Cancel Karo Ya Renew Karo" AI Assistant
Kya karega: Har renewal se 7 din pehle user ko email bhejega:
"Zoom Pro ₹1,500/month — last used: 45 days ago. 80% team Google Meet use kar rahi hai. Recommendation: CANCEL. Potential savings: ₹18,000/year."
Kyun killer hai: Baki tools sirf data dikhate hain, yeh decision bhi batayega. User sochne ki zaroorat nahi — seedha action le sakta hai.
Monetization: Pro plan mein daalo — ₹12,000/month wale log yeh feature ke liye pay karenge.
2. 💰 "Paisa Bachao" Guarantee
Kya karega: Dashboard pe ek banner dikhega:
"WasteDetect ne aapka ₹47,000 bachaya hai. Aapne humko ₹5,000 diye. Net savings: ₹42,000."
Kyun killer hai: Har mahine user ko exact ROI dikhta rahega. Churn impossible ho jayega — kyunki paisa bach raha hai toh koi cancel nahi karega!
Monetization: Har plan mein free — yeh retention ka weapon hai.
3. 📧 "Email Forward = Auto Add" Magic
Kya karega: User kisi bhi software ki invoice email ko add@wastedetect.com pe forward karega. System:
PDF read karega
Amount nikaalega
Date nikaalega
Subscription auto-add ho jayega
Kyun killer hai: Manual entry ki tension khatam. Baki tools mein ya toh bank connect chahiye (trust issue) ya manual entry (time waste). Yeh beach ka rasta hai.
Monetization: Growth plan se start — Starter mein manual entry rahegi.
